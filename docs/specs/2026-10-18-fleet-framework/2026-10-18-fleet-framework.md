## What this is

The shared meaning that three products build against: the fleet, people, workspaces, members, capabilities, personas, agents and bots, channels, and permissions.

- This set holds the shared **Requirements** and the shared **Specification**. The Specification owns the nouns.
- Each product writes its own Program Design and Verification Design against the Specification's entity ids, in its own spec:
  - agent-vm: `docs/specs/2026-10-14-portable-tool-portal-hosts/`;
  - agent-collaboration: its repo;
  - AgentStudio daemon: `agentstudio.feat-daemon/docs/specs/2026-10-15-agentstudio-daemon/`.

## Who builds what (handoff map, proposed)

| Product | Builds |
|---|---|
| **agent-collaboration** | the fleet registry (people, workspaces, members, personas, agents, bots); **channels and their permissions**; mailboxes; the inbox classifier; the workspace chief; session attestation |
| **agent-vm**, specifically its Tool Portal and MCP Portal (F-36) | MCP tools for workspace members; the **registration and configuration that let a persona pick its tool subset**, by building out the Tool VM configuration; credentials and the broker; tool approvals |
| **AgentStudio daemon** | Hosts and runtime nouns (its spec E1–E26), referencing fleet ids rather than redefining them; clients. Excluded until settled are the identity, authorization, human-message and naming overlaps: E6/E17 ([Q-C3](open-questions.md)), E18 (Q-B3), E11 (Q-D5) and E25 (Q-B2) |

The daemon Advisor proposed the detailed noun-ownership split at concept-thread post 8458. It is an input to the Specification.

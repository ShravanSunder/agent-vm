## Verified substantive findings

### TA1 — Admission cannot construct the operation record from its declared input

**Important; accepted. Program Design; architecture-boundary.**

Governing U3/U6 require exact approved intent and reconnectable work; Specification E6/R5/R7 require exact arguments, stable item identity and conflict detection. [PD198](/Users/shravansunder/code/projects/agent-vm.design-portable-tool-portal-hosts/docs/specs/2026-10-14-portable-tool-portal-hosts/program-design.md:198) defines `PortalApprovalIntent` as key, fingerprint, revisions, surface and expiry. [PD259](/Users/shravansunder/code/projects/agent-vm.design-portable-tool-portal-hosts/docs/specs/2026-10-14-portable-tool-portal-hosts/program-design.md:259) gives `admit(intent)` responsibility for creating a record. But [that record](/Users/shravansunder/code/projects/agent-vm.design-portable-tool-portal-hosts/docs/specs/2026-10-14-portable-tool-portal-hosts/program-design.md:225) also requires capability, canonical arguments and optional logical-session association, none supplied by that intent. The sequence's admission message at PD144 likewise contains only key/fingerprint/revisions.

Failure: the declared authority boundary receives a hash without the work needed to populate the record or later render the promised exact approval challenge at PD327–329. A hash cannot recover arguments. A planner must add an unstated payload or side channel. The current source avoids this: [existing protected intent](/Users/shravansunder/code/projects/agent-vm.design-portable-tool-portal-hosts/packages/tool-portal/src/standalone-entrypoint/standalone-tool-portal-approval.ts:17) carries the full qualified call/arguments, and the [service constructs it](/Users/shravansunder/code/projects/agent-vm.design-portable-tool-portal-hosts/packages/tool-portal/src/standalone-tool-portal-service.ts:377).

Smallest correction: complete the admission request shape at this existing boundary with the canonical invocation and optional non-authoritative association needed by the record; specify their validation/fingerprint relationship, and align the sequence/interface/binding/trace cells. Do not introduce a second store or request-history service.

Route: **program-design**.

Confirmation: every persisted record/challenge field can be traced to admitted input or an explicitly authority-derived value; changed arguments conflict and correlation remains outside intent equality. Reverify E6/E7/E9, PD144/198–240/259/327–329 and R3/R5/R7/R9 trace cells. Deleting durable admission would violate the rails; completing its existing payload is the smallest correction inside scope. No contested evidence or missing-source dependency.

### TA2 — The arm contract leaves an expired reserved approval eligible to send

**Important; accepted. Program Design; failure-concurrency/security-trust.**

U3 and Specification R5/C2 preserve exact current, expiring authorization; July R54/R54a require expired attempts to remain not-dispatched. [PD261](/Users/shravansunder/code/projects/agent-vm.design-portable-tool-portal-hosts/docs/specs/2026-10-14-portable-tool-portal-hosts/program-design.md:261) rechecks assignment/policy at arm and lists no expired rejection. The [state view](/Users/shravansunder/code/projects/agent-vm.design-portable-tool-portal-hosts/docs/specs/2026-10-14-portable-tool-portal-hosts/program-design.md:429) handles expiry while Approved, but Reserved→Armed requires only current policy and a one-use transition.

Concrete interleaving: reserve/consume while approval is valid; pause beyond `expiresAt` without a crash; arm under unchanged assignment/policy. The written guard permits a send under expired approval. Crash/incarnation handling does not cover a still-live delayed owner. The existing [controller arm path](/Users/shravansunder/code/projects/agent-vm.design-portable-tool-portal-hosts/packages/agent-vm/src/controller/approval/controller-approval-ledger.ts:607) checks expiry, including another check after asynchronous input-authority validation at 624–626.

Smallest correction: make approval expiry a required arm-time guard before issuing the permit, with a closed expired/no-send outcome and matching reserved-state/failure/proof treatment. Preserve consumed authority; do not recycle it or add retry machinery.

Route: **program-design**.

Confirmation: simulate expiry between reserve and arm with zero external sends, alongside concurrent arm and current-revision checks. Reverify PD208/261/429–434/451–477 and R5's proof seam. Deleting approvals or omitting the guard weakens the governing obligation; this is an existing-authority correction inside scope. No contested evidence.

### TA3 — Portable artifact authorization and storage are not bound to a usable interface

**Important; accepted. Program Design; architecture-boundary/security-trust.**

The R14 trace row names “result/artifact owner” without resolving this mechanism in the component/interface tables.

The foundation is concrete, not automatically portable: [existing storage interface/limits](/Users/shravansunder/code/projects/agent-vm.design-portable-tool-portal-hosts/packages/gateway-runtime/src/artifacts/artifact-store.ts:57) define writer/range/remove operations and four quotas; [existing authorization](/Users/shravansunder/code/projects/agent-vm.design-portable-tool-portal-hosts/packages/gateway-runtime/src/artifacts/artifact-read-authority.ts:15) uses a managed principal plus capability, fingerprint, operation/generation and surface. Its live-authority comparison is at 268–288. The new E2 envelope at PD21 instead uses authority/issuer/subject/credential identity. The standalone projection already has a [distinct read-caller contract](/Users/shravansunder/code/projects/agent-vm.design-portable-tool-portal-hosts/packages/tool-portal/src/standalone-entrypoint/standalone-tool-portal-mcp-projection.ts:110).

Failure/downstream ambiguity: reusing the managed authorization shape directly requires inventing managed-principal fields; replacing it informally leaves generation/current-authority checks and byte-storage quotas to the planner. Neither follows from “protected bindings.”

Smallest correction: bind artifact responsibility to the existing shared-service boundary, name the reused portable storage interface/home and its Node/Worker realization, and define the public-host authorization record/read mapping while retaining the managed adapter's existing identity/epoch rules. Show the bounded successful and rejected read/write paths. This does not require durable artifacts, a new blob provider, or a new public resource API.

Route: **program-design**.

Confirmation: a public caller can read its admitted artifact bytes and cannot read another caller's, stale-generation or expired data; storage bounds and retirement have a named enforcement owner. Reverify PD79–104/529–534 and R11/R12/R14 rows against the existing interfaces. Deleting artifact access would weaken preserved behavior; binding the existing seam is the smallest in-scope correction. No missing Specification entity is demanded and no contested evidence remains.

### TA4 — The public call result is not mapped to the newly introduced operation states

**Important; accepted. Program Design; contract.**

U2/U6/U9 and Specification R1/R7/R9/R12 require equivalent normalized state/results, stable recovery and TS/Python discriminant parity. [PD210–240](/Users/shravansunder/code/projects/agent-vm.design-portable-tool-portal-hosts/docs/specs/2026-10-14-portable-tool-portal-hosts/program-design.md:210) defines internal admission/operation states, while [PD243](/Users/shravansunder/code/projects/agent-vm.design-portable-tool-portal-hosts/docs/specs/2026-10-14-portable-tool-portal-hosts/program-design.md:243) reuses the existing call-item result schema and PD275–296 specifies input envelopes. The normalized public result binding for an existing approved/reserved/armed/running/result-expired item, its ordered batch wrapper and its relationship to get/cancel results is not defined.

The [current call-result schema](/Users/shravansunder/code/projects/agent-vm.design-portable-tool-portal-hosts/packages/agent-portal-sdk/src/portal-call-surface/models/portal-call-result-schema.ts:140) has ok/error/approval_required item variants, not these new nonterminal states. Both [TS client parsing](/Users/shravansunder/code/projects/agent-vm.design-portable-tool-portal-hosts/packages/agent-portal-sdk/src/tool-portal-mcp-client/index.ts:191) and [Python parsing](/Users/shravansunder/code/projects/agent-vm.design-portable-tool-portal-hosts/python/agent-vm-agent-portal-sdk/src/agent_vm_agent_portal_sdk/tool_portal_mcp_client.py:133) consume that authored public result contract. Failure: two implementers can return a raw operation state, a new call-item status, or an error for the same running duplicate; clients disagree or reject it. A permissive completed payload can also misclassify pending/ambiguous work.

Smallest correction: define the versioned public invocation/get/cancel result bindings, including batch item identity/order, using the already specified operation states and existing bounded terminal outcomes. Explicitly restrict the completed payload to the appropriate terminal subset. Carry the same authored schemas/refinements into both clients; introduce no second result taxonomy.

Route: **program-design**.

Confirmation: each operation state and rejection maps to exactly one valid public representation in both presentations/languages; completed/pending and completed/ambiguous contradictions are invalid. Reverify PD184–296/448–449 and R1/R7/R8/R9/R12/R14 trace cells. Removing status/recovery would violate the rails; completing this mapping is the smallest in-scope correction. No contested evidence.

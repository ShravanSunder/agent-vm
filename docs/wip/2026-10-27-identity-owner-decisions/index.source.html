Your questions, answered

Nothing to pick here. These stay as designed unless you tell me otherwise.

"What do you mean step 2 breaks login?"

It isn't expected to. Step 2 is the switch where Fleet central accepts only the new login and each machine logs in again. It's rehearsed on test deployments first. "Fixes forward" only says what happens if a bug gets through anyway: we fix it and deploy, rather than switching back to the old login.

"Token family carries organization and enrollment": what it means

A machine keeps itself logged in by trading a refresh token for new tokens at the sign-in service. The refresh token on its own only says who approved it, and that's you for every machine. So it can't tell your machines apart or stop just one. We put a short note on it, "org X, approval Y", which Better Auth copies onto each new refresh token. Every trade checks that approval:

The two you ticked without a comment

A machine is claimed for an organization only after you approve it. A machine belongs to one organization for good. If that were recorded when it merely asked to log in, a login you denied would still lock it into that organization.

Logging a machine in again cuts off its old connection. If you log a machine in again, perhaps because its old login leaked, the old login stops working at once. Its sessions and workspaces carry over, because they belong to the machine.

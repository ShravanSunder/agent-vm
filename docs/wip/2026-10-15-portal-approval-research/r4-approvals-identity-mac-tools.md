## 1. Managed approval flow and who approves

**Answer — FACT, high confidence:** the host controller owns the durable approval record and one-use admission. Hermes owns presentation and its native actor admission. The controller's recorded `approverId` is the zone's one configured managed-gateway authority label; it is not the Telegram/Discord/API user's authenticated identifier. A decision is fenced to the original **agent principal**, while native human identity stays inside Hermes.
